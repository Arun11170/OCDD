# Crop Advisor: what it does and what it does not do
*Version 0.1, October 2026. For farmers, KVK staff and pilot partners.*

## What it does
- Looks at a photo of a leaf, fruit or pest and suggests what the problem may be, with a confidence. It works **without internet**.
- Shows field steps (cleaning, removing plants, water, rotation), organic options, and a **checked list of products with no doses**.
- Helps decide "spray or wait", sets a re-check reminder, and asks later "better, same or worse?"
- Works in English and 12 Indian languages (the translations are machine-made, labelled beta, and still being reviewed by native speakers).

## Measured accuracy (dataset test photos, 1500 held out)
- Right on the first guess: **92%** of all photos, **88%** of field-style photos.
- When the app is sure enough to answer (about 92% of photos): right about **96%** (field-style: 93%).
- Weakest groups: rice (74%), chilli (85%), okra (88%), pests (84%). For these the app says "not sure" more often.
- **These are not yet real-farm numbers.** A test with KVK-labelled real field photos is planned and real accuracy will probably be lower.

## What it does NOT do
- It does not give doses, mixing amounts or a prescription. Always read the product label.
- It cannot cure virus diseases; it says so and suggests removing plants and controlling the insects that carry them.
- It does not replace a KVK or agriculture officer. When it is unsure, when damage is severe, or when the plant gets worse, it asks the farmer to call the free **Kisan Call Centre 1800-180-1551** or show the plant to a person.
- Weather and crop-stage cards are simple rules from what the farmer taps, not a forecast.
- "Smart" question answers (optional download) are English only and are checked against the app's own advice.
- Reminders can arrive a few minutes late.

## Privacy
Photos, answers and history stay on the phone. The only network use is the one-time optional download of the Smart model started by the farmer.

## Status of advice
Advice is taken from TNAU, ICAR and state-university pages (sources shown in the app). Product names were checked against CIB&RC lists dated 31.03.2026 and 31.07.2026, name only; label claims for each crop and pest are **not** checked. A KVK / agronomist review is in progress.
