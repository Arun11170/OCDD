## 1. Summary
Offline crop disease and pest detection with a Llama advisory, on an Arm Android phone. Two separate pipelines: vision (ONNX Runtime) and advisory (Llama 3.2 1B through ExecuTorch).

## 2. Architecture
- Vision pipeline: photo -> EfficientNet-V2-S (224 px, ONNX Runtime) -> structured findings (crop, problem, confidence, top-3, "not a plant" flag) -> leaf/lesion segmentation + OpenCV -> damage % and band.
- Advisory pipeline: findings + verified knowledge-base entry (kb.json / kb_hi.json, official Indian sources) -> prompt -> Llama 3.2 1B Instruct (QLoRA INT4, ExecuTorch, XNNPACK, separate Android process) -> output check -> advisory.
- Why two pipelines: different tasks; vision can be improved without touching the advisor; the Llama process can be killed to free ~1.9 GB without affecting detection.
- Llama deployment: `.pte` from Meta's pre-quantized release; `org.pytorch:executorch-android` library; `LlmModule` in `LlmService` (process `:llm`); temperature 0.2.

## 4. Advisory grounding and safety
- Llama sees only the verified facts; dose questions are refused; output checker blocks numbers, chemicals, organisms and disease names not in the facts; failure falls back to the verified text.
- Languages: Llama writes English and Hindi (Llama 3.2 officially supports Hindi among Indian languages); other languages use the reviewed translated text. TODO(Hindi review by a native speaker: result).
- Advice quality test: TODO(30 advisories scored pass/fail against the knowledge base; number passing; examples of blocked answers).

## 5. Uncertainty handling
Three states: confident / unsure (extension-worker prompt) / cannot judge (blurry, wide view, not a plant). Severe damage always adds the extension-worker prompt. Every advisory states it is a likely cause, not a confirmed diagnosis. TODO(counts from the failure photo bank).

## 8. Limitations
- Field accuracy is lower than lab accuracy (TODO numbers); pests are the weakest group; look-alike pests are merged.
- Llama 1B can be wrong; the checker reduces but cannot remove that risk, hence the fallback and the expert prompt.
- Languages other than English/Hindi are not generated by Llama.
- Needs about 2 GB free RAM for the advisor; weaker phones get the verified text only.
