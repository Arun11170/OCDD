## 1. What it does
A farmer or extension worker photographs a leaf, fruit or insect with an Arm Android phone. Entirely on the device the app (1) names the likely disease or pest, (2) measures how much of the leaf is damaged, and (3) writes practical advice in a local language, with an extension-worker prompt when it is unsure or the damage is severe. No internet is used in operation (docs/OFFLINE_AUDIT.md).

## 2. Architecture: two separate pipelines
**Vision pipeline** (ONNX Runtime, CPU): photo -> EfficientNet-V2-S classifier (224 px) -> temperature-scaled probabilities -> per-crop confidence gate -> findings (crop, problem, confidence, top-3, not-a-plant flag) -> leaf segmentation and lesion segmentation + OpenCV -> damage % and severity band (very light / mild / moderate / severe).

**Advisory pipeline** (ExecuTorch, Arm CPU): findings + region + verified knowledge-base entry -> prompt -> **Llama 3.2 1B Instruct (QLoRA INT4, 1.18 GB .pte) through ExecuTorch**, in its own Android process -> output checker -> advisory. The separate process means a low-memory kill takes down only Llama, never the detector, and unloading returns ~1.9 GB at once.

Why separate: image recognition and text generation are different tasks; the vision model can be improved without touching the advisor; each is tested on its own. No Hailo accelerator is used (the advisory runtime is ExecuTorch on the Arm CPU, the vision runtime is ONNX Runtime on the CPU).

## 3. Grounding and safety of the advisory
- Llama sees only verified facts for the detected problem (cause, symptoms, field steps, when to act, treatments named by official Indian sources) plus the measured damage and the farmer's region. Temperature 0.2.
- Dose and mixing-rate questions are never sent to Llama; the app refuses and refers to the Kisan Call Centre (1800-180-1551) and the product label.
- Every Llama answer is checked before display: numbers not in the facts, chemicals, organisms and disease names not in the facts, over-long answers, and (for non-English) wrong script, English words, or long words absent from the verified text in that language. A failed answer is replaced by the verified text, so an unchecked answer is never shown. While Llama is writing, the verified text is already on screen.
- Languages: English and Hindi are generated by Llama (Hindi is the only Indian language Llama 3.2 officially supports). The other 11 languages (BN, TE, MR, TA, GU, UR, KN, OR, ML, PA, AS) are tried too, with the strict checker, and a language shows Llama text only if the on-device benchmark shows it passes: TODO(table of pass rate per language from tools/summarize_metrics.py). ## 4. Data and training
- Sources: PlantDoc, PlantVillage, Mendeley sets (groundnut, chilli, okra, rice, tomato), IP102 (pests), and Kaggle sets for mango, sugarcane, maize, banana; licences and obligations in docs/DATA_LICENSES.md (some sets are CC BY-NC: hackathon/research use only). TODO(finish the licence VERIFY column).
- 14 crops plus pests; 95 classes plus a "not a plant" class (96). Negative class built from COCO val2017 scenes, DTD textures and degraded copies of real leaf photos (blurry, dark, tiny in a large scene, blown out). Splits by photo group to avoid leakage: train 76,759 / val 15,316 / test 15,561 (with negatives). Field photos and lab photos are reported separately.
- Training: ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNet-V2-S, 20 epochs, batch 32, lr 5e-4, rare-class sampling boost (power 0.75), temperature calibration. int8 quantisation was rejected (it lost far too much accuracy), so the app ships fp32 (81 MB).

## 5. Vision results
Shipped model (run5), held-out test (14,262 photos): accuracy 0.911, macro-F1 0.889; **field photos 0.863 (n=9,188), lab photos 0.997 (n=5,074)**; calibration error (ECE) 0.023.
Confidence gate (answer only if confidence >= threshold): at 0.80 the model answers 92.3% of photos and is right 94.9% of the time when it answers (0.90: answers 88.1%, right 96.4%).
Independent 1,500-photo check (accuracy_lab.py) with the shipped file: top-1 0.921 (field 0.878); at gate 0.80 it answers 93.1% and is right 0.951 (field: answers 89.6%, right 0.920). Weakest groups: pests (right 0.905 when answering) and chilli; the most-swapped pairs are look-alike pests (leafhopper/planthopper, rice leaf folder/stem borer).
Healthy leaf called affected: 3.4%; affected leaf called healthy: 1.2% (run5 test).

## 8. Limitations
- Field accuracy (0.86-0.88) is below lab accuracy (0.997); the app compensates by saying "not sure" instead of guessing, at the cost of coverage.
- Pests are the weakest group; look-alikes are merged where experts cannot tell them apart from a photo.
- A 1B model can be wrong or vague; the checker lowers but cannot remove the risk, so verified text is the fallback and every result carries "likely cause, not a confirmed diagnosis".
- Llama needs about 2 GB of free RAM; weaker phones get the verified text only.
- Non-commercial data licences restrict commercial use of the trained model.
- Machine-translated text for 11 languages has not all been reviewed by native speakers.
